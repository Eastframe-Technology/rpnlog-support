Frequently asked

Is there an equals key? No. RPNLog uses RPN, like the classic financial calculators: key a number, press ENTER, key the next number, then the operation. The quick guide (the ? button) shows the common keystrokes.

My payment has the wrong sign. RPNLog follows the cash-flow sign convention: money you receive is positive, money you pay is negative. A loan you receive is a positive PV, so the payment comes out negative.

n came out one higher than I expected. As on RPN financial calculators, a computed number of periods is rounded up to the next whole period (unless the fraction is under 0.005), because the last, smaller payment still counts as a period.

How do I key in a date? As M.DDYYYY (for example 11.022026 for 2 November 2026), or D.MMYYYY after tapping the date chip on the display.

Is there a subscription? No. RPNLog Pro is a single purchase. Restore it on a new phone from Settings.

Privacy policy

What we collect: nothing. RPNLog has no account system, no analytics, no advertising, and no crash reporting service. It never connects to the internet and sends no data to us or anyone else.

Purchases are handled by the App Store or Google Play. We do not see your payment details.
