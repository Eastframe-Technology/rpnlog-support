Privacy policy

Effective 2 November 2026. RPNLog is published by Eastframe Technology LLC.

What we collect: nothing. RPNLog has no account system, no analytics, no advertising, and no crash reporting service. It never connects to the internet and sends no data to us or anyone else.

Your numbers (the stack, registers, cash flows, tape and settings) are stored on your device until you uninstall the app. If you export a CSV file, it goes to the app or service you choose in the share sheet.

Purchases are handled by the App Store or Google Play. We do not see your payment details.

Third parties. The app contains no third-party advertising or analytics code. The only external pages it opens are this page and the store's rating page, and only when you tap them.

Children. RPNLog is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes. A new version of this policy will be published at this address with a new effective date.
