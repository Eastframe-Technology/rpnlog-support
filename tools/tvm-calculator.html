How it is calculated

The five values must balance. Every TVM problem is this one equation, solved for whichever value is missing:

PV × (1 + i)^n + PMT × (1 + i × S) × ((1 + i)^n − 1) ÷ i + FV = 0

i is the rate per period as a decimal (0.5% is 0.005). S is 0 for END and 1 for BEGIN. When i is 0, the middle part is simply PMT × n.

PV, PMT and FV have direct formulas. N has one too, using logarithms, and is then rounded up to a whole period. I/Y has no direct formula once there is a payment, so it is found by searching for the rate that makes the equation balance, starting with the sign change closest to zero.

Worked examples

Monthly mortgage payment

450,000 borrowed for 30 years at 6.5% a year, paid monthly: N = 360, I/Y = 6.5 ÷ 12 = 0.5417, PV = 450,000, FV = 0. The answer is PMT = −2,844.31.

Savings goal

100 a month for 10 years at 0.5% a month, from nothing: N = 120, I/Y = 0.5, PV = 0, PMT = −100. The answer is FV = 16,387.93. You pay in, so PMT is negative. You collect at the end, so FV is positive.

Finding a rate

A loan of 30,000 repaid at 600 a month for 5 years: N = 60, PV = 30,000, PMT = −600, FV = 0. The answer is I/Y = 0.6183% a month, about 7.42% a year. Note that this is the rate per month times 12, not a compounded yearly rate.

Limits

  • The rate stays the same for all periods, and it compounds once per period. A rate that compounds on a different schedule needs converting first.
  • Payments are all equal and evenly spaced. For uneven payments you need cash flows and IRR, which is in RPNLog Pro.
  • It does not include fees, taxes or insurance, so a lender's APR or total monthly bill can differ.
  • If a problem has more than one rate that fits, it returns the one closest to zero.
  • Rates from about −99.99% to 10,000% per period are searched.

Frequently asked

Why is my payment negative? It follows the cash-flow sign convention: money you receive is positive and money you pay is negative. The loan comes to you, so PV is positive. The payments leave you, so PMT is negative. The guide on the TVM sign convention covers more cases.

Is I/Y the yearly rate? No. Here it is the rate per period, the way classic RPN financial calculators work. For a monthly loan at 6.5% a year, enter 6.5 and press ÷12, which gives about 0.5417. N is also in periods: 30 years of monthly payments is 360.

Why did N come out as a whole number? A computed N is rounded up to the next whole period unless the fraction is under 0.005. The last payment, smaller than the others, still counts as a period. For example, 1,000 at 1% per period paying 30 needs 41 periods.

Why do I get "No solution"? Usually PV, PMT and FV all have the same sign, so money only flows one way and no rate or term can balance it. It also happens when the payment is smaller than the interest on the loan, so the balance never falls.

What is the difference between END and BEGIN? END means payments arrive at the end of each period, as on most loans. BEGIN means at the start, as on many leases and rents. A BEGIN payment is the END payment divided by (1 + rate per period).
